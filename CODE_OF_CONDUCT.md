# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Cert Sphere, part of the Media Sphere Open Source Community, aims to provide a welcoming, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone who participates in project spaces and activities.

We welcome contributors regardless of experience level, background, identity, or preferred way of contributing.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, considerate, and constructive.
- Give feedback about ideas and code, not personal characteristics.
- Accept reasonable criticism and differing technical opinions.
- Assume good intent while addressing harmful behavior directly.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Focus discussions on what is best for the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, sexualized harassment, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Publishing private information without permission or using project spaces to target or abuse others is prohibited.

## Scope

This code applies to project repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces when someone is representing or participating in the Cert Sphere project community.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to either:

- [mediasphere680@gmail.com](mailto:mediasphere680@gmail.com)
- [mdatheeq.2004@gmail.com](mailto:mdatheeq.2004@gmail.com)

Please include relevant context or links where safe to do so. Do not post sensitive personal information publicly. Maintainers will review reports as fairly and confidentially as reasonably possible, investigate where appropriate, and take action proportionate to the circumstances. Possible actions include a warning, request to stop the behavior, temporary restriction, or removal from project spaces.

The project does not tolerate retaliation against people who raise concerns in good faith. If a report concerns one contact, use the other contact.

## Maintainer responsibility

Maintainers are responsible for explaining and applying these standards consistently. This policy is intended to support a healthy community, not to prevent respectful disagreement or good-faith technical debate.
